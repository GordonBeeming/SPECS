A local steel line was built, then replaced by an import

Between the two snapshots above, Elevator Yard briefly smelted its own steel to cover Steel Beam demand: an Iron Ingot line, a Steel Ingot line and a Steel Beam line, plus coal, iron and copper claims of its own, taking it to a peak of 9 machines and 104.3 MW. Once the Steel Mill's export cap was raised to cover the full 30/min the Yard needed, all three local lines and their claims came out, dropping the factory to 3 machines and 35.4 MW before the Versatile Framework assembler went back in. Net effect: 6 machines and 53.9 MW built and then removed within the group, none of it visible in the before/after numbers above. It's the clearest example in this run of why pulling an intermediate over a logistics link beats smelting it locally — the Yard never needed its own ore claims at all.
