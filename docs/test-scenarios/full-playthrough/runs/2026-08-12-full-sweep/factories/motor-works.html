Motor Works — Tier 4

Run: 2026-08-12-full-sweep · Map position: 1.5 km W · 1.5 km N · Claims: coal Normal 37.5%, copper Normal 26%, iron Pure 52%, all Miner Mk.1 · Power: 128.5 MW draw (no local generation)

Purpose

Tier 4's mechanical parts factory: Stator 5/min, Motor 2.5/min and Automated Wiring 2.5/min, sized for the Tier 4 milestones. 20 machines plus 3 extractors, 128.5 MW draw, fed by the grid rather than its own generators — Motor Works sits close enough to the Coal Power Station to run entirely off it. The optimizer routed both ingot lines through the same alternates as Steel Mill — Iron Alloy Ingot and Solid Steel Ingot — rather than reusing Motor Works' own copper claim for a base-recipe wire line.
