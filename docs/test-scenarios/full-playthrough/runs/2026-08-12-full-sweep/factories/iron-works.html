Iron Works — Tier 0

Run: 2026-08-12-full-sweep · Map position: 1.7 km W · 1.3 km N · Claims: iron #N6 and #N13, both Miner Mk.1 at 50% · Power: 30 MW gen / 28 MW draw (+2 MW)

Purpose

The starter iron line. Makes Iron Plate 20/min, Iron Rod 20/min and Screws 40/min — everything the Tier 0 and Tier 1 milestones ask for in iron, and the plates and rods the Space Elevator's first phase needs. Nothing leaves the factory yet; Tiers 1–2 pick the outputs up over logistics links once there's somewhere to send them.

Why two miners at 50% rather than one at 100%

A miner feeds a single belt, and a splitter after the port can only divide what already came through it. One Normal node at 100% is 60/min, which saturates a Mk1 belt exactly and leaves the second smelter to be fed by a splitter off that same 60. Two nodes at 50% give 30/min each instead: every segment runs at half capacity, each smelter has its own miner, and there is no splitter in the ore path at all. The spare headroom on both nodes is what Tier 1 upgrades into when Mk2 belts arrive.
