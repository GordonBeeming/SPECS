Copper Works — Tier 0

Run: 2026-08-12-full-sweep · Map position: 1.4 km W · 1.0 km N · Claims: copper Normal at 50%, limestone Pure at 37.5%, both Miner Mk.1 · Power: 30 MW gen / 21 MW draw (+9 MW)

Purpose

Everything Tier 0 needs that isn't iron: Wire 30/min, Cable 15/min and Concrete 15/min. Wire and concrete are what the early milestones and the first Space Elevator phase eat; cable is the one the HUB asks for by hand long before anything automates it.

Why the clocks look odd

Both are chosen to land on an exact rate rather than a round percentage. Copper sits on a Normal node at 50% for 30/min, which is precisely what one smelter eats. Limestone sits on a Pure node at 37.5% for 45/min, which is precisely what one concrete constructor eats. A Pure node at 100% would be 120/min — double the Mk1 belt — and clocking it down is cheaper than building a second belt to throw most of it away.

The cable constructor runs at 50%. Cable is the only product here that consumes another product of the same factory, and 15/min is half what a constructor makes, so the alternative is a full-rate machine idling half the time.
