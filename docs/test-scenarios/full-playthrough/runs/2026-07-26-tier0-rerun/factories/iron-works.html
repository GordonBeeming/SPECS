Iron Works — Tier 0

Run: 2026-07-26-tier0-rerun · Grass Fields, around 1.5–1.9 km W · 0.5–1.2 km N · Claims: Iron Pure #1 (1.5 km W · 0.5 km N) Miner Mk.1 @ 50% = 60/min, Iron Normal #6 (1.9 km W · 1.2 km N) Miner Mk.1 @ 100% = 60/min · Power: +30 MW gen / −24 MW draw = +6 MW

Purpose

The starter iron line. Feeds the Tier 0 milestone costs directly — Iron Plate 20/min, Iron Rod 20/min and Screws 40/min, all consumed on site or by hand. Nothing is exported: at Tier 0 both factories are self-contained, so there are no logistics links. Reinforced Iron Plate is deliberately absent — it needs an Assembler, which is Tier 2.

Only one of the two claimed nodes is actually drawn on: the plan needs 60 iron ore/min and each node supplies 60/min. The second node is claimed headroom for the Tier 1–2 expansion, which is why the app reports "120/min claimed" against a 60/min draw.

The belt-cap choice

A Pure iron node on a Miner Mk.1 at 100% yields 120/min, which is double what a Mk1 belt carries. Both fixes the tier page names are used here, one per node, so the layout shows the trade rather than hiding it:

  • Underclock — Iron Pure #1 runs at 50%, giving exactly 60/min onto a single Mk1 belt. No splitter, no second belt, and the miner draws less power.
  • Claim a Normal node — Iron Normal #6 yields exactly 60/min at 100% with no clock fiddling at all.

Splitting a 120/min Pure node across two parallel Mk1 belts is not a third option at all: a miner feeds one belt, so 60/min is all that reaches the splitter in the first place. Underclocking gets the same 60/min without wasting half the miner's clock.
