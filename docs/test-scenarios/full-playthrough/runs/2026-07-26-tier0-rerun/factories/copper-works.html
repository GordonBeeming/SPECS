Copper Works — Tier 0

Run: 2026-07-26-tier0-rerun · Grass Fields, 1.7 km W · 1.3 km N · Claims: Copper Normal #8 (1.7 km W · 1.3 km N) Miner Mk.1 @ 100% = 60/min, Limestone Pure #3 (1.7 km W · 1.3 km N) Miner Mk.1 @ 37.5% = 45/min · Power: +30 MW gen / −17.6 MW draw = +12.4 MW

Purpose

The copper and concrete line — Wire 30/min, Cable 15/min and Concrete 15/min. Everything is consumed on site or by hand for milestone costs; there are no exports, because Tier 0 has no logistics links.

The copper and limestone nodes share the same map coordinates, which is why this factory sits where it does: both extractors are on the doorstep and the longest belt run in the whole build is a few foundations.

Rate derivation

Cable is the reason Wire runs hot. The recipe takes 2 Wire per Cable (60 Wire → 30 Cable), so 15 Cable/min eats 30 Wire/min on top of the 30 Wire/min that is itself a product. Wire therefore produces 60/min total, which needs 30 Copper Ingot/min and so 30 Copper Ore/min — half of what the Normal copper node yields.

Concrete is the opposite shape: it is limestone-hungry and machine-cheap. 45 Limestone → 15 Concrete/min on a single Constructor. The limestone node is a Pure one (120/min at 100%), so it is underclocked to 37.5% to land exactly on 45/min instead of running three times the needed rate into a belt that could not carry it anyway.
